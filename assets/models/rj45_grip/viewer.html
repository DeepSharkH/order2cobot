RJ45 grip shell

Shell and snap-on bottom cover for a latch-cut plug. A Robotiq Hand-E drops its fingertips into the two notches of the H; the flared leg ends funnel them in. It closes on the crossbar and pushes the plug horizontally into the switch port.

The orange shell and green cover are the actual STLs, printed as two parts. Plug, cable, switch face and fingertips are reference shapes for checking clearance. The drawing uses third-angle projection and is generated from the same numbers as the STL.
