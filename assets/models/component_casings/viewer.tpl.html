Component casings

Every part rides in the same kind of block: 25 mm tall, split through the lead plane and glued, with the leads leaving the bottom on the 2.54 mm grid. The Hand-E grips the block, never the part. Jumpers reuse the original 5 mm jumper casing's channels, moved to each spacing.

Halves and parts are the actual geometry from casings.py: the orange half is half 1 (y < 0), the teal one half 2. Print each half lying on its outer face, split face up, no supports. Parts are standard sizes: 1/4 W resistor, 1N4148, 5 mm LED, TO-92, 5 mm disc, 5 × 11 mm electrolytic. The sparks are for show only.
